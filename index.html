WHO WE ARE

Mineral resources.
Meaningful connections.

Evolve Africa Minerals is an emerging African minerals enterprise focused on mineral sourcing, commodity trading, supply-chain development and mineral processing.

With an initial focus on opportunities within South Africa's minerals sector, we are building relationships across producers, suppliers, processors and buyers. Our wider ambition is to develop an integrated platform that supports value creation throughout the mineral supply chain.
